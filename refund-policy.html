Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective July 29, 2026

Free trials

Trial availability, duration, included features, and any payment requirements will be disclosed before activation. You will not be charged solely for sending a trial request. When payment information is required, the billing date and renewal terms will be shown before you authorize a paid subscription.

Subscription cancellation

You may request cancellation at any time by emailing ben@lsintegrations.com. Unless otherwise stated during checkout, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period, and access continues through that period.

Refunds

Because SCO is a digital subscription service, completed subscription charges are generally non-refundable except where required by law. We will review requests involving duplicate charges, incorrect billing, unauthorized charges, or a material service failure attributable to SCO.

Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically at the disclosed billing frequency until canceled. To avoid the next renewal charge, submit the cancellation request before the renewal date shown in your billing information.

Plan changes

Upgrade or downgrade timing, proration, and any resulting credits or charges will be disclosed when the plan change is requested or made available through the account.

How to request help

Email ben@lsintegrations.com with the account name, billing email, charge date, amount, and a brief description of the request. Do not send full payment-card numbers by email.
