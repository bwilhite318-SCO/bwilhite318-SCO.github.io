What to know before starting

What types of companies can use SCO?

SCO is intended for service companies and other organizations that create, assign, track, and complete work orders. It is not limited to a single trade.

Is SCO available now?

SCO is currently being offered in early access. Trial availability, onboarding steps, and current feature availability are confirmed when you contact us.

Does SCO support multiple companies?

Yes. SCO is being designed as a multi-company platform, with each subscribing company maintaining its own account data, users, customers, work orders, and settings.
