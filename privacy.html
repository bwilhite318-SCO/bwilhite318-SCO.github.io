Privacy Policy

Effective July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Service Call Organizer (“SCO,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), operated by LS Integrations, handles information when you visit our website, request a trial, or use our software and related services.

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with SCO, we may collect:

  • Contact information, such as your name, company name, email address, phone number, and business address.
  • Account information, including login credentials, user roles, company settings, and subscription status.
  • Service data entered into SCO, such as customer records, service locations, work orders, job notes, assignments, and status information.
  • Technical information, such as browser type, device information, IP address, usage events, error logs, and security-related activity.
  • Billing-related information. Payment card details may be processed by a third-party payment processor and are not intended to be stored directly by SCO.

How we use information

  • Provide, operate, maintain, secure, and improve SCO.
  • Create and manage accounts, trials, subscriptions, and customer support requests.
  • Process transactions and communicate about billing, service changes, and account activity.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and technical problems.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate SCO, such as cloud hosting, database, email, analytics, payment, security, and customer-support providers. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transaction such as a merger or asset sale.

Customer data and company accounts

Business customers control the service data they enter into SCO. Authorized users within the same company account may be able to access that company’s data according to assigned roles and permissions. Customers are responsible for determining what information they enter and for obtaining any notices or permissions required for their own customers, workers, or contractors.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by data type and account status.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security, and users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their credentials and reporting suspected unauthorized access.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of certain personal information by contacting us. Some records may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting purposes.

Children

SCO is a business service and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the website or service.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as SCO develops. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or by email when appropriate.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to ben@lsintegrations.com.

Review note: This policy is a practical operating draft for SCO and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before broad commercial launch.
